# US46 #120: Suspend and Restore Accounts

As an administrator, I want to suspend an account that breaks the community guidelines so that it can no longer use the site.

Depends on: US39. Estimate: 2 points.

## Acceptance criteria

- Staff can suspend or restore an account with a required reason. Each successful action records the actor, time, and reason.
- Suspension sets `is_active` false, closes the account's active ride posts, and holds its visible fare reports.
- A suspended account sees a suspension message only after the correct email and password; a wrong password gets the usual generic error.
- Restoration permits sign-in again. It does not reopen posts or republish fare reports.
- Staff cannot suspend their own account or the last active staff account.
