# #76 US29: Surge-Pricing Overlay
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As a commuter, I want to see the hours when Uber and Lyft are usually pricier than normal so that I know when a yellow cab is likely the better deal.

Tier: Nice-to-Have · Depends on: US27, US3

**Developer Tasks**
- Agree with the Product Owner on the "likely surge" rule. Proposed: an hour where the zone's average rideshare fare per mile is at least 1.25 times that zone's median fare per mile across the week, with at least MIN_TRIP_COUNT trips
- Add GET /api/zones/:id/surge, returning for each of the 7 x 24 cells whether it is a likely surge hour, plus the fare per mile and average wait
- Add a "Show likely surge hours" toggle on the heatmap that marks those cells, with a legend entry
- Explain in the UI that this is an estimate, because real surge multipliers are not public
- Record the rule and the endpoint in docs/decisions.md and the High Level Design

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Turning on the overlay marks the likely surge hours on the selected zone's heatmap
- Hovering over a marked cell shows its fare per mile and average wait
- The overlay can be turned off, and the legend explains how surge is estimated
- Cells with too few trips are never marked as surge

The rule and decisions are in `docs/decisions.md`, "Our choices (surge-pricing overlay, US29 #76)".
