# #94 US32: Find Shared Rides on My Route
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As a signed-in commuter, I want to see open ride-share posts on a route like mine around my time so that I can find someone to share with.

Depends on: US31, US18

### Developer Tasks
* Add a "Find a shared ride" page at /rides/: pickup zone, dropoff zone and date are required; the time is optional
* A post matches when its pickup is the same zone or an adjacent zone (from zone_neighbors.json, US18), its dropoff is the same or an adjacent zone, its departure is on the chosen date, and, if a time is given, within 1 hour of it
* List only open posts that are not expired, not full and not the rider's own, with exact matches first, then by departure time
* Show for each post: the owner's display name, pickup and dropoff zones, departure time, seats left, the note, and "Same route" or "Nearby pickup/dropoff"
* Add a "Find a shared ride" link to the trip summary, next to "Report a fare", with the zones and the selected day filled in
* Sign-in required to see posts
* Record the matching rule in docs/decisions.md

### Acceptance Criteria
* Searching a route and date shows open posts on the same route and on adjacent zones, exact matches first
* With a time given, only posts within 1 hour of it are listed
* Full, closed, expired and the rider's own posts are not listed
* Each post shows the owner's display name, never an email address
* If nothing matches, the page says so and offers "Post a shared ride" with the same route and date
* Guests see a sign-in prompt in place of the list
