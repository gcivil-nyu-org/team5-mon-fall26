# #99 US37: My Rides
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As a signed-in commuter, I want one page with my posts, the rides I've asked to join, and new messages so that I don't miss anything.

Depends on: US34, US36

### Developer Tasks

- Add a "My rides" page at /rides/mine/ with two lists: "My posts" (status, seats left, interested riders) and "Rides I've asked to join" (status: pending, accepted, declined, closed or deleted)
- Show the number of unread messages for each conversation, and mark messages as read when the conversation is opened
- Add "My rides" to the nav, with a small badge showing the total number of unread messages or new interested riders
- Hide rides more than 7 days in the past by default, with a "Show past rides" option

### Acceptance Criteria

- "My rides" lists the user's own posts and the rides they've asked to join, each with its current status
- Unread message counts are shown, and they clear when the conversation is opened
- The nav badge shows new messages and new interested riders, and disappears when there are none
- Past rides are hidden unless "Show past rides" is chosen
