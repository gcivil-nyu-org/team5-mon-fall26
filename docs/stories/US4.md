# #6 US4: Filter by Time
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As a visitor, I want to filter by day of the week and hour of the day so that I can see taxi patterns for the time I plan to travel.

Developer Tasks
- Add a day-of-week selector and an hour slider
- Store the selected day and hour in the app state
- Re-query and refresh the map, fare range, and trip duration when the filters change

Acceptance Criteria 
- The user can choose any day of the week and any hour of the day
- The map colors, fare range, and trip duration update to match the selected time
- The selected day and hour are always visible on screen
- The filters default to the current day and hour
