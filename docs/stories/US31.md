# #93 US31: Post a Ride-Share Offer
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As a signed-in commuter, I want to post that I'm taking a taxi from one zone to another at a certain time so that riders going the same way can share it with me.

Depends on: US30, US8

### Developer Tasks
* Add a RidePost model: owner (on_delete=CASCADE), pickup zone, dropoff zone, departure date and time (New York time, 15-minute steps), seats offered (1 to 3), optional note (up to 200 characters), status (open, full or closed), created date
* Add a "Post a shared ride" form at /rides/new/, opened from the trip summary with the pickup and dropoff filled in; guests see "Sign in to share a ride"
* Validate: different pickup and dropoff zones; the departure is in the future and no more than 14 days ahead
* Show a warning on the note field not to include addresses, phone numbers or other contact details
* Limit each user to 3 open posts at a time
* A post whose departure time has passed counts as expired: work this out when the posts are read, so no scheduled job is needed
* Record the rules in docs/decisions.md and the High Level Design

### Acceptance Criteria
* A signed-in user with a display name can post a ride, and it appears as "Open"
* A departure in the past, more than 14 days ahead, or with the same pickup and dropoff zone is rejected with a clear message
* A 4th open post is rejected with a message
* A post whose departure time has passed is shown as expired and no longer counts as open
* Guests see a sign-in prompt in place of the form
* Deleting the account deletes the user's posts

The decisions are in `docs/decisions.md`, "Our choices (post a ride-share offer, US31 #93)".
