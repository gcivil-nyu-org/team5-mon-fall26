# #118 US44: Review Fare Reports Held for Review
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As an administrator, I want to approve or remove fare reports held for review so that real prices are shown and fake or mistaken ones are not.
Depends on: US39, US24
Estimate: 2 points
## Developer Tasks
- Add a "Removed" status to FareReport alongside "Visible" and "Held for review"; removed reports never appear in FareReport.objects.public()
- In the Django admin, show held reports first, each next to the route's historical average fare for that day and hour
- Add "Approve" (makes it visible) and "Remove" actions, with an optional reason, recording who acted and when
- Show the rider "Removed by a moderator" on My fare reports (US25), and don't let them edit a removed report back to visible
## Acceptance Criteria
- Held reports are listed first, with the historical average to compare against
- Approving a report makes it count in the rider reports summary (US26)
- Removing a report hides it from every public view, and its owner sees that it was removed
- Every approval and removal records the admin and the time
