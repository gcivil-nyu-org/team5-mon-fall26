# US45 #119: Moderate Reported Posts and Messages

As an administrator, I want to act on reported ride posts and messages so that ride sharing stays safe.

Depends on: US39, US38. Estimate: 3 points.

## Acceptance criteria

- Staff can remove a reported post. It disappears from search and its detail page for everyone; interested riders see "Removed by a moderator" in My rides.
- Staff can hide a reported message. The message stays in the database, while both the conversation page and polling endpoint show a removal notice instead of its text.
- Every closed report records "Content removed" or "No action needed", who closed it, and when. Removing content closes all open reports on that item.
- Open reports appear first, with the number of reports on each item. The reported rider never sees the reporter's identity.
- Deleting the reported rider's account still cascades to their posts, messages, and the reports about them.
