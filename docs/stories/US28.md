# #75 US28: Compare Taxi and Rideshare Fares for My Trip
Status on GitHub: OPEN

As a commuter, I want to see the usual taxi fare next to the usual Uber/Lyft fare for my trip and time so that I can pick the cheaper option.

Tier: Nice-to-Have · Depends on: US27, US8

**Developer Tasks**
- Add GET /api/routes/rideshare?from=&to=&day=&hour=, reading RideshareRouteHourStats, with the same validation and enough_data rule as /api/routes
- Show a "Taxi vs. rideshare" section in the trip summary: fare range and typical duration for each, plus the typical rideshare wait time
- Say which was historically cheaper for that day and hour, and by how much, only when both sides have enough data
- Label it as based on past trips, not live prices (a rideshare app's live price can be higher during a surge)
- Record the endpoint in docs/decisions.md and the High Level Design

**Acceptance Criteria**
- With both zones selected, the trip summary shows taxi and rideshare fares and durations side by side
- It says which option was historically cheaper and by how much
- Each side shows "Not enough data" on its own when it has fewer than 5 trips
- The section updates when the zones, day or hour change
- The result is labeled as historical, not live prices

The decisions are in `docs/decisions.md`, "Our choices (taxi vs. rideshare fares, US28 #75)".
